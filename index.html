Who We Are

About Dugnad School

Discover our educational vision and the values behind the Dugnad School concept.

Our Background

Dugnad School is presented as a proposed nursery and primary school concept in Wakiso District, Uganda. It is designed around the idea of providing a supportive environment where children can learn, discover their abilities and develop important life skills.

Our Vision

To nurture knowledgeable, confident and responsible learners who can contribute positively to society.

Our Mission

To provide quality foundational education through effective teaching, creativity, discipline and individual learner support.

Our Core Values

Integrity: Doing what is right.
Respect: Valuing others.
Excellence: Striving to improve.
Creativity: Encouraging new ideas.
Responsibility: Being accountable.

Primary Three

Primary Four

Primary Five

Guidance and Support

Our Teachers

The proposed teaching approach places emphasis on guidance, patience and learner participation.

Supporting Every Learner

The proposed Dugnad teaching team is intended to guide learners through engaging lessons, classroom activities and individual support.

Teachers are expected to encourage questions, creativity, good behaviour, teamwork and confidence in every learner.

The aim is to create a positive relationship between teachers, learners and parents.

Admissions

Information for parents and guardians interested in the proposed Dugnad School programme.

Admission Requirements

Parents and guardians should prepare the following documents:

  1. Completed application form.
  2. Birth certificate or proof of age.
  3. Recent passport-size photographs.
  4. Previous school records, where applicable.
  5. Relevant health and immunisation information.

For admission enquiries, contact the Academic Registrar.
