About Dugnad School

Learning through practical activities, creativity and holistic early childhood development.

Our Background

DUGNAD is a contemporary, fast-growing Kindergarten and Day Care located in Kavule–Jomayi Estate along Matuga–Semuto Road in Wakiso District, about an hour's drive from Kampala.

The school is dedicated to providing an activity-based curriculum aimed at producing a holistic child through formal and informal Early Childhood learning programmes.

At DUGNAD, every child is considered unique and is encouraged to reach their potential. Through a talented and experienced team, the school promotes lifelong learning based on foundational skills and critical thinking.

Facilities and services designed to support children's learning, well-being and safety.

A Place to Learn and Grow

A good learning environment supports children's development, curiosity and well-being.

Dugnad's facilities and services include classrooms, water, electricity, internet access and toilet facilities for children and staff.

The school also provides a feeding programme, security arrangements, transport and learning resources.

Admissions

Dugnad School welcomes applications for Daycare, Kindergarten and Primary.

Admission Requirements

Children are admitted to Daycare from the age of six months, Kindergarten from three years and Primary from six years.

In some situations, a test of the child's numeracy and English literacy skills is required to assess the appropriate class.

Documents Required

  • Two passport-size photographs of the child.
  • Two passport-size photographs of the parents.
  • A copy of the child's birth certificate.
  • A copy of the child's immunisation card.

Admission Fee

Each child pays an admission fee of UGX 50,000.

Interested parents can obtain application forms from the Head Teacher's office or contact the school administration.
